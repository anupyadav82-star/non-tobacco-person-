Health recovery timeline
20 minutes
Heart rate aur blood pressure nicotine ke attack se normal ki taraf aane lagte hain.
12 hours
Blood me carbon monoxide level normal hota hai. Har saans me zyada oxygen milti hai.
2–12 weeks
Walking, सीढ़ी, दौड़ — sab asaan lagne lagta hai. Circulation aur lung function strong.
3–9 months
Khansi, सांस फूलना, wheezing kam. Lung ki सफाई system (cilia) wapas full power pe.
1 year
Heart attack ka risk almost आधा ho jata hai ek smoker ke comparison me.
5–10 years
Stroke aur mouth / throat cancer ka risk bahut kam ho jata hai. Aapka शरीर aapko reward de raha hai.
Ye generic timeline hai. Har इंसान की health अलग hoti hai – doctor se regular check-up jaroor karein.
Withdrawal & urges toolkit
2–5 minutes rule
Jab भी urge aaye, stopwatch lagao: sirf 2–5 minute survive karna hai. Har urge ek लहर ki tarah hota hai – aata hai, peak karta hai, fir चला jata hai.
Replace ritual
Bike chalate waqt ya खाने के baad agar हमेशा gutkha tha, ab वही time ek नया ritual: पानी, च्युइंग gum, deep breathing, ya 20 squats.
Emergency plan
3 चीज़ें हमेशा ready rakho:
1) किसी का नाम jise call kar sakte ho,
2) छोटी walk,
3) ye app kholna aur “+1 urge defeated” दबाना.
